Still Arrows · Privacy

Your quiet is yours.

Effective November 13, 2026

Still Arrows is an offline puzzle game published by Md Imam Hosen. The game does not use ads, analytics, accounts, or social features.

Information stored on your device

Puzzle progress, settings, completed-board dates, undo totals, and your daily-play streak are stored locally using Android DataStore. This information is not sent to the developer. The game does not access contacts, photos, location, calendar, or microphone.

One-time purchase

Still Pass is processed by Google Play. Google handles payment and purchase records under its own privacy policy. The app checks whether a purchase is active to unlock content and restore it on another device signed in to the same Play account. The app never receives payment card details.

Offline play

The puzzles, soundscapes, settings, and saved progress work offline. Google Play may need a network connection to complete or restore a purchase. The app checks Android's local network status only to show its offline-session badge; the check is not sent to the developer.

Children

Still Arrows is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. It has no advertising, social features, or user-generated content.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. Google manages purchase records. The developer does not keep a copy of local game progress.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Md Imam Hosen through the developer contact address shown on the Google Play listing.
